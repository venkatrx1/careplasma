Available Jobs

Open roles at our New York City center

Physician Substitute (LPN/RN)

Under supervision of the Center Manager, Physician, and Quality Assurance Supervisor, and in compliance with Care Plasma's Standard Operating Procedures and State/Federal regulations, this role ensures donor suitability, donor safety, and product integrity.

Qualifications: EMT-P, LPN, or RN licensure in NY State (must be maintained); good oral and written communication skills; computer proficiency; CPR certified; able to stand/walk for a full shift; able to lift up to 5 lbs regularly, occasionally up to 32 lbs, and rarely up to 50 lbs.

Benefits: 401(k), health insurance, paid time off.

Phlebotomist

Responsible for the efficient and effective collection of plasma from donors — performing venipuncture, setting up the plasmapheresis machine, and monitoring the pheresis process in line with our Standard Operating Procedures.

Qualifications: High school diploma or equivalent; phlebotomy certification and venipuncture experience preferred; strong communication with donors; able to set up, operate, and troubleshoot plasmapheresis equipment; standing/walking up to 100% of shift; occasional lifting up to 25 lbs.

Plasma Processor

Under supervision of the Center Manager and Quality Assurance Supervisor, this role processes, stores, packs, and ships plasma units and samples, and performs necessary equipment calibrations with thorough documentation.

Qualifications: High school diploma/equivalent; good oral and written communication; computer proficiency; able to stand/walk for a full shift; able to lift up to 50 lbs; able to work short periods in plasma freezers colder than -30°C.

Donor Screener

Under supervision of the Operations Supervisor and Quality Assurance Supervisor, this role determines donor suitability for plasmapheresis in compliance with our Standard Operating Procedures and State/Federal regulations.

Qualifications: High school diploma/equivalent; good oral and written communication; computer proficiency; able to stand/walk for a full shift; able to lift up to 5 lbs regularly, occasionally up to 32 lbs, and rarely up to 50 lbs.
