Compensation

Donate And Get Paid!

Earn up to $500 in your first month

As a first-time donor at Care Plasma, you can earn up to $500 in your first month — and a substantial amount as a returning donor.

How To Get Started

Three simple steps to your first payout

First, make sure you meet the requirements on our eligibility page. Once you have your documents ready, you're welcome to walk in — no appointment needed.

1

Bring your documents. A government-issued photo ID, proof of address, and your Social Security card.

2

Walk in or book ahead. Visit 1400 5th Avenue during operating hours — no appointment required.

3

Donate and get paid. Your Paysign card is loaded after every successful donation, ready to use immediately.

How You Are Going To Be Paid

Your Paysign Visa debit card

After a successful donation, you'll receive a reloadable Care Plasma Paysign Visa debit card. It's refilled with your earnings after every visit — spend it anywhere Visa is accepted, just like any other debit card. Enjoy your extra cash right after each donation.
