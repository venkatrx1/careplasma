Our Vision

A commitment to quality, safety, and community

At Care Plasma, we are dedicated to improving lives by collecting high-quality source plasma that is used to manufacture life-saving plasma-derived therapies. Every donation has the potential to help patients living with rare, chronic, and life-threatening medical conditions, making our donors true partners in advancing healthcare.

Our mission is to provide a safe, compassionate, and exceptional donation experience while maintaining the highest standards of quality, integrity, and regulatory compliance. We believe that every donor deserves respect, every patient deserves hope, and every plasma donation can make a meaningful difference.

As an FDA-licensed plasma collection organization and a PPTA International Quality Plasma Program (IQPP) certified company, Care Plasma is recognized for its commitment to quality, safety, and operational excellence. Our quality management systems are designed to ensure that every unit of plasma collected meets or exceeds all applicable FDA regulations and industry standards, giving our healthcare partners and patients confidence in the products we provide.

Our state-of-the-art plasma collection centers are designed with donor comfort and safety in mind. Supported by experienced healthcare professionals and highly trained staff, we provide a welcoming environment where every donor receives exceptional care throughout the donation process. We continuously invest in our people, technology, and quality systems to enhance the donor experience while ensuring the highest level of product quality.

Innovation and continuous improvement are fundamental to our success. We are constantly evaluating new technologies, refining our processes, and implementing best practices to improve operational efficiency, strengthen quality systems, and support the evolving needs of the plasma industry.

As part of our long-term vision, Care Plasma is actively expanding its operations to serve more communities and increase the availability of high-quality source plasma. Through strategic growth, investment in modern facilities, and a commitment to excellence, we are building a stronger organization capable of meeting the increasing global demand for plasma-derived therapies.

At Care Plasma, we measure our success by the positive impact we make on the lives of patients, the trust we earn from our donors, the dedication of our employees, and the strength of the communities we serve. Guided by integrity, compassion, innovation, and excellence, we are committed to advancing the plasma industry while expanding access to life-saving therapies for patients around the world.

A New York City plasma donation center helping patients access life-saving therapies — one donation at a time.
