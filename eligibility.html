Required documents

Government-issued photo ID ▾

  • State-issued photo ID
  • U.S. Passport
  • Foreign Passport
  • Permanent Resident Card / Green Card
  • Military ID

  • Student ID with photo

  • Bank Statement

  • Lease / Rental Agreement
  • Property Tax Statement

  • Vehicle Registration

  • Government Correspondence

Who Can Donate

Donor criteria

Age & weight

18 years or older, at least 110 lbs (50 kg), with a healthy hemoglobin level.

Travel

Recent travel to certain high-risk regions may result in a temporary deferral.

Medication

Some medications — certain antibiotics, blood thinners, immunosuppressants — may affect eligibility. Ask our staff about yours.

Lifestyle

Certain lifestyle factors or recent exposure to infectious disease may lead to deferral.
